## PART 1: PRIORITIZATION FRAMEWORK

### Scoring Methodology (Culi.Flow KPI Pattern)

Each of your 20 prompts scored across 4 dimensions:

**1. URGENCY** (1-5)
- 5 = Crisis (unpaid wages, FMLA dispute)
- 4 = Time-sensitive (job applications, interviews)
- 3 = Important but flexible (portfolio development)
- 2 = Strategic (business planning)
- 1 = Future-focused (cryptocurrency, real estate)

**2. IMPACT** (1-5)
- 5 = Life-changing (wage recovery, job offers)
- 4 = Major advancement (Executive Chef position)
- 3 = Significant improvement (portfolio quality)
- 2 = Meaningful progress (networking, brand)
- 1 = Incremental value (system optimization)

**3. ENERGY REQUIRED** (1-5)
- 1 = Low cognitive load (templates, checklists)
- 2 = Moderate focus (documentation review)
- 3 = Sustained attention (writing, analysis)
- 4 = High complexity (business planning)
- 5 = Intensive deep work (legal documentation)

**4. DEPENDENCIES** (Boolean)
- Blocks other prompts = TRUE
- Independent work = FALSE

### Priority Score Formula

```
Priority Score = (Urgency × 2) + (Impact × 1.5) - (Energy × 0.5) + (Dependencies × 3)
```

**Rationale:**
- Urgency weighted 2x (crisis demands immediate action)
- Impact weighted 1.5x (results matter more than activity)
- Energy weighted -0.5x (higher energy = lower immediate priority during stress)
- Dependencies weighted +3 (unblock other work first)

## PART 6: RISK MITIGATION

### Energy Crash Protocols

**If overwhelmed during Crisis Phase:**
1. Return to #20 (72-Hour Plan)
2. Pick ONE action from "Now" lane
3. Set 25-min timer
4. Complete that action only
5. Take 10-min break
6. Assess: continue or stop for day

**If decision paralysis during Application Phase:**
1. Use Command Palette → "apply"
2. System selects highest priority application
3. Load template from #3
4. Fill in company-specific details only
5. Submit without perfectionism
6. Log and move to next

**If isolation/disconnection during Network Phase:**
1. Use template from #5/#19
2. Pick 3 easiest contacts (warm relationships)
3. Send identical message to all 3
4. Log activity
5. Celebrate sending (outcome doesn't matter yet)

### Flexibility Protocols

**If Prompt #X isn't working:**
- Skip to next prompt in same phase
- Return to skipped prompt after 48 hours
- If still blocked, escalate to weekly review
- Consider breaking prompt into smaller sub-prompts

**If timeline slips:**
- Phases are guidelines, not deadlines
- Assess: is it avoidance or legitimate constraint?
- Adjust Phase 2-6 timelines as needed
- NEVER adjust Phase 1 (crisis can't wait)

## PART 8: DAILY OPERATING RHYTHM

### Morning Ritual (30 minutes)

**Time:** First 30 min of workday
**Location:** Quiet space, coffee/tea, notebook

**Steps:**
1. Review yesterday's EventLog (5 min)
2. Check KPI Dashboard (Crisis/Application/Financial) (5 min)
3. Identify today's CRITICAL task from current phase (5 min)
4. Identify 2-3 HIGH tasks for today (5 min)
5. Set 3 time blocks: CRITICAL (2h), HIGH (1h), ADMIN (30m) (5 min)
6. Write "Today I will..." statement (5 min)

**Example:** "Today I will [file DoL complaint] (CRITICAL), [submit 2 Executive Chef applications] (HIGH), and [email Chef Carl] (HIGH)."

### Focus Block Structure

**CRITICAL Block (2 hours, morning):**
- 25 min work → 5 min break
- 25 min work → 5 min break
- 25 min work → 5 min break
- 25 min work → 10 min break
- Total: 100 min work, 20 min breaks

**HIGH Block (1 hour, late morning):**
- 50 min work → 10 min break

**ADMIN Block (30 min, afternoon):**
- Email, logging, planning, quick hits

### Evening Shutdown (15 minutes)

**Time:** Last 15 min of workday
**Location:** Same as morning

**Steps:**
1. Log today's events to EventLog (5 min)
2. Update KPI Dashboard (3 min)
3. Move tasks to tomorrow or backlog (3 min)
4. Write "Tomorrow I will..." statement (2 min)
5. Close all work tabs/apps (2 min)

**Example:** "Tomorrow I will [tailor resume for Sodexo role] (CRITICAL), [prep for Compass interview] (HIGH), and [update LinkedIn with portfolio piece] (MEDIUM)."

### Weekly Review (60 minutes)

**Time:** Sunday evening or Monday morning
**Location:** Strategic thinking space

**Agenda:**
1. Review week's EventLog (all entries) (10 min)
2. Update Analytics Dashboard (4 panels) (10 min)
3. Assess phase progress vs. plan (10 min)
4. Identify blockers and risks (10 min)
5. Plan next week's CRITICAL path (10 min)
6. Celebrate wins (5 min)
7. Adjust priorities if needed (5 min)
